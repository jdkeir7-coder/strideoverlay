Show metrics

Cadence shows steps per minute across both feet. One-leg GPX readings are doubled; files already reporting both feet stay as recorded. If the file has no cadence readings, it stays hidden. Pace and speed can be calculated from your route.

Choose a graph of the current part of your run or a larger route map showing your live position, start and finish.

You can also click the overlay in the preview, then drag its corners to resize it or its sides to stretch it.

A PNG with a transparent background works best. It stays on this device and is added to the exported video.

Shows a 4 second summary of the lap (distance, time, pace, heart rate) at each lap, then returns to the normal overlay.

Colours follow % of max HR: blue easy, green steady, lime moderate, orange hard, red very hard. Set your measured max if known.
